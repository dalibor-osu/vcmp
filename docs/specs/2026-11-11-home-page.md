# Home page

Status: agreed
Date: 2026-11-11
Area: vcmp-app (Home page and its Show all pages, sidebar, navigation, start page,
Settings), vcmp-library (queries over the play log, added dates and favourites);
docs (DESIGN.md, ui.md, library.md, manual-testing.md)

## Context of the change

VCMP opens on the Albums grid, and no page shows what was listened to. The listening
spec ([2026-10-22-listening-and-library-features.md](2026-10-22-listening-and-library-features.md))
planned a Home page and decided most of its shape; issue 79 carries it. Its two
dependencies are built and merged: play history (issue 77) and favourites (issue 78).

What exists today:

- **The play log.** `plays` holds one row per counted play: track, start time and
  origin text (`crates/vcmp-library/src/plays.rs`,
  [library.md, Play history](../library.md#play-history)). `Library::play_stats`
  gives every track's count and last play, over all time or since a moment.
- **Origins.** The origin is the context key the app gave the engine, stored as it
  is: `album:<id>`, `artist:<id>`, `playlist:<id>`, `favourites`, `label:<id>`,
  `folder:<path>`, `search`, `songs`, `agent:<name>`, or NULL for a track taken from
  Up next and for a play with no context (`crates/vcmp-app/src/player_service.rs`,
  `src/player_service/agent_playback.rs`). Ids inside the text can stop resolving: a
  playlist or label can be deleted, and a tag edit that regroups tracks can give an
  album a new id ([library.md](../library.md), tag writing). The play history spec
  ([2026-11-07-play-history.md](2026-11-07-play-history.md)) left it to this change
  whether Home uses plays without an origin.
- **Favourites.** `favourites` holds track and time liked
  (`crates/vcmp-library/src/favourites.rs`).
- **Added dates.** `tracks.added_ms`, backfilled once from file dates; `Track`
  exposes it.
- **No queries for Home yet.** Nothing reads recent origins, albums by added date,
  plays per album, or liked tracks not played since a moment.
- **No Home, no start-page setting.** The window starts on Albums
  (`selected-index: 1` in `crates/vcmp-app/ui/app-window.slint`). `ui.start_page`
  and `ui.most_played_range` do not exist.
- **Sections are addressed by number.** The `Page` enum and a `nav` list exist, but
  `selected-index`, `show-section(index)`, the `section()` table in
  `crates/vcmp-app/src/navigation.rs` and the screenshots example and five test files
  use positions (35 uses in 8 files), so adding a first section shifts every one of
  them.
- **The navigation history is in memory only** (`src/navigation.rs`,
  [ui.md, Back and forward](../ui.md#back-and-forward)): up to 100 places, nothing
  saved when VCMP closes.
- **Cards and rows.** The card grids (`AlbumGrid`, `CardGrid` in
  `ui/album-grid.slint`) show albums, artists and playlists; a `CardGrid` grows to as
  many rows as its tiles need. Tracks are only ever shown as rows (`TrackList`,
  `SongTable`).
- **Search is the precedent for a page that opens other pages.** A result opens on
  its own section's page and Back returns to Search
  ([ui.md, Search](../ui.md#search)).
- **The sidebar has three groups** by the owner's decision of 2026-10-18
  ([DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md)): Search; Albums to Folders; Settings and About.
- **The setup wizard** has a short Welcome that lists no features, and a start-page
  step was ruled out ([2026-11-03-first-run-setup-wizard.md](2026-11-03-first-run-setup-wizard.md)),
  so this change does not touch the wizard.

The listening spec's decisions for Home stand here unchanged: the four sections, one
row each with Show all, Home first and the default start page, the Most played
ranges and their picker, the 3-month rule, the empty states, the refresh rule and
pages addressed by name. The issue restates them; they are listed under "User
request" below.

Left open by the earlier specs: whether Most played shows albums, tracks or both and
how its Show all page looks, and what Recently played does with plays that have no
origin.

## User request

The owner asked to brainstorm the topic explained in GitHub issue 79, "Home page with
recently played, recently added, most played and forgotten favourites", and to
analyze it. The issue says:

VCMP opens on the Albums grid and has no place that shows what you have been
listening to. The listening spec plans a Home page. It depends on play history (77)
and favourites (78). Wanted, as that spec decided:

- Home is the first section of the sidebar.
- A start page setting, `ui.start_page`, either `home` (the default) or `last`.
  "Last" reopens the last place in the navigation history, or Home if it no longer
  exists.
- Sections, one row each with a Show all page. Empty sections are hidden, and a Home
  with nothing to show offers "Add music folder..." like the other pages:
  - Recently played: play origins, newest first.
  - Recently added: albums ordered by their newest added date.
  - Most played: plays in a chosen range: last week, last month, last year or all
    time (rolling 7, 30 and 365 days). The same range picker sits in the section's
    heading on Home and on its Show all page, both following `ui.most_played_range`
    (default last month, kept after a restart). Its heading and picker stay even
    when the range is empty.
  - Forgotten favourites: liked tracks with no play in the last 3 months, including
    tracks never played since they were liked once they have been liked for 3
    months.
- Refresh: the sections are library queries, refreshed when Home opens and after a
  play is recorded, never on position events.
- Navigation: the `Page` enum and navigation stop using hard-coded indices, so
  adding a section is a one-place change; tests refer to pages by name.

Left open by the spec, to settle here and record in `docs/ui.md`: whether Most
played shows albums, tracks or both, and how its Show all page looks.

Out of scope per the spec: sideways-scrolling shelves, recommendations beyond the
four sections, much-played albums in Forgotten favourites, custom date ranges.

Also asked for: the new sidebar order in `docs/DESIGN.md` as the owner's decision;
tests for each section's query (ranges, the 3-month rule), the start-page setting and
navigation by page name; `docs/ui.md`, `docs/manual-testing.md` and screenshots of
Home in both themes; every new string through `@tr()`.

## Decisions

### What is one entry in Home's Recently played row, given that plays are logged from albums, artists, playlists, Favourites, labels, folders, search, Songs, agents and Up next?

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| Played from, else album (Recommended) | A play from an album, artist, playlist, Favourites or label shows that card; any other play (search, Songs, folder, Up next, agent) shows its track's album. Album cards follow the track's current album, so a tag edit can't drop them. Nothing played goes missing; the row mixes card shapes and a Songs shuffle fills it with single-track albums. |
| Only what has a page | Album, artist, playlist, Favourites and label plays show as cards; plays from search, Songs, folders, Up next and agents leave no trace. A row of deliberate choices only; an album queued with Add to queue never appears, and an album id replaced by a tag edit still has to be found through its track. |
| Albums only | Every play shows its track's album, wherever it started. One card shape and no ids to resolve; a playlist or artist you played from never appears, and a shuffled playlist shows one album per track. |

**Answer: Played from, else album**

### What does Most played show: albums, tracks or both, on Home and on its Show all page?

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| Albums on Home, both on Show all (Recommended) | Home's row is album cards ranked by the plays of their tracks in the range; Show all has the top albums as a grid and the top tracks as a list with play counts, behind an Albums/Tracks switch. Home stays rows of cards; the songs you replay most are a click away, not on Home itself. |
| Both on Home | A row of album cards with the top five tracks listed under it; Show all shows both in full. Most at a glance; the tallest section on Home, and it pushes Forgotten favourites further down. |
| Tracks only | The top tracks with their play counts: about five rows on Home, a long list on Show all. Exactly what the play log counts; a list among card rows, and no view of your most played albums until the statistics page. |
| Albums only | Album cards on Home, a grid on Show all. One shape everywhere and the least to build; nothing shows which songs you replay most until the statistics page. |

**Answer: Albums on Home, both on Show all**

### Forgotten favourites are liked tracks, but Home is rows of cards and VCMP has cards only for albums, artists and playlists. How does this section show on Home?

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| A short track list (Recommended) | About five rows of the usual track list, with hearts, the track menu and the keyboard as everywhere; Show all is the full list. Tracks look like tracks everywhere in VCMP; it is the one section on Home that isn't a row of cards. |
| Track cards | Each track is a card with its album's cover, its title and artist, in a row like the others. Home is cards throughout; a new kind of card to design (what a click does, no heart or length), and two tracks of one album show the same cover twice. |
| Their albums as cards | The row shows the albums that hold forgotten favourites, and Show all lists the tracks. Nothing new to build on Home; the row shows albums, not the liked tracks the section is about. |

**Answer: A short track list**

### When there are more forgotten favourites than Home's section holds, which ones does Home show? (Show all lists every one, longest unplayed first.)

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| A different handful each day (Recommended) | A pick from all of them that changes with the date and holds still during the day. Rediscovery stays fresh; the section is less predictable, and a track you saw yesterday may not be there today. |
| Longest unplayed first | The most forgotten on top, the same ones until you play them. Predictable and simplest; the section shows the same tracks for weeks if you keep passing them by. |

**Answer: A different handful each day**

### Home has track lists of its own that can be played (Forgotten favourites, the top tracks on Most played's Show all). What does a play started there show as in Recently played?

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| Its track's album (Recommended) | The lists are contexts of their own (the queue says "Next from Forgotten favourites"), and their plays follow the rule for search and Songs: the track's album shows. Home never lists itself; five forgotten tracks played in a row put five albums in the row. |
| A card for the list | Recently played gets a Forgotten favourites card, and a Most played one, that open their Show all page. You can get back to the list you played; Home's first row then points at Home's own sections. |
| The Favourites card | Plays from Forgotten favourites count as played from Favourites. No new context to add; the queue and the row say Favourites while only the forgotten ones play, and the top tracks list still needs a rule of its own. |

**Answer: Its track's album**

### The sidebar has three groups by your decision of 2026-10-18: Search; Albums to Folders; Settings and About. Where does Home go?

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| Top group, above Search (Recommended) | Home, then Search, share the first group; the library and the app's pages follow as today. Home is truly first, as the spec says, and the sidebar keeps three groups; Search moves down one place from where it has always been. |
| Heads the library group | Search stays alone on top and Home comes above Albums. Search doesn't move; Home is not the first entry, and it sits in a group whose other entries are all views of the library. |
| Alone on top | Home gets a group of its own above Search, making four groups. Home stands out most; one more divider in a sidebar that already scrolls in a short window. |

**Answer: Top group, above Search**

### Where in Settings does the start-page choice (open on Home, or on the last page) sit?

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| Appearance (Recommended) | With the theme, zoom and reduce motion: what the window looks like when it opens. No new section; Appearance is otherwise about look, not behaviour. |
| New General section | A heading of its own near the top with this one row. Clearest, and room for later choices of this kind; a whole section for one setting. |
| Top, by Run setup again | A row beside the Setup row at the top of the page. Seen first; that spot is about the wizard, and two unrelated rows sit under no heading. |

**Answer: Appearance**

### Addressing pages by name instead of by index touches about 100 call sites in 19 files (mostly tests), and CI is off. In how many pull requests does the Home page land?

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| Two: navigation, then Home (Recommended) | The index-to-name change lands alone first, with nothing visible changing, then Home on top of it. The mechanical renames can't hide a Home bug and each review is readable; one more merge and one more full local run of the checks. |
| One | Navigation, the queries, Home and the start-page setting on one feature branch. One merge and one run of the checks; about 100 mechanical renames are reviewed together with the feature. |
| Three: navigation, queries, Home | The library queries with their tests also land on their own before the page. Smallest reviews; queries are merged before anything uses them, and two more merges while CI is off. |

**Answer: One**

Note, after the answer: the figure in the question was too high. A count of the
positional forms only (`selected-index`, `show-section` and their Rust names) gives
35 uses in 8 files; the other matches already named pages. The answer stands.

### Forgotten favourites: a track you last played four months ago and liked yesterday. Is it forgotten today? (The 3 months count as 90 days in both options.)

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| No: play and like both old (Recommended) | Forgotten only when its last play and its like are both over 90 days old, so a fresh heart never lands straight in the section. A track you just liked waits 90 days; the section stays about music you chose and then let slip. |
| Yes: only the last play counts | Forgotten when not played in 90 days, whenever it was liked; never-played liked tracks still wait 90 days after the like. Fills sooner, and an old song you re-heart shows at once; a heart given today can put a track in 'forgotten' the same day. |

**Answer: No: play and like both old**

## High-level plan

One pull request from one feature branch, as the owner decided. The workstreams are
built in this order, and the full checks run after the first one, before anything is
built on top of it.

**1. Pages by name.** The window, the history and every test name a section by its
`Page` instead of its position. Nothing visible changes. Depends on nothing; it comes
first so that putting Home on top renumbers nothing.

**2. Library queries.** Read-only queries with their tests: the play log's recent
entries, albums by newest added date, albums and tracks ranked by plays in a range,
and forgotten favourites. Depends on nothing.

**3. Home page.** Home as the first section, in the top group with Search: the four
sections, their empty states and refresh rules, cards that open their pages and
return to Home, and the Forgotten favourites list as a play context. Depends on 1
and 2.

**4. Show all pages.** One per section, each a place in the history. Most played's
has the range picker and the Albums/Tracks switch. Depends on 3.

**5. Start page.** The choice in Settings, Appearance; the last place saved and
reopened. Depends on 1 and 3.

**6. Documentation and screenshots.** DESIGN.md (the sidebar order as the owner's
decision, new visuals under "Awaiting the owner's review"), ui.md (a Home section,
the sidebar and start rows of its decisions table), library.md (the queries),
manual-testing.md, README.md, the listening feature's implementation notes, and Home
in every theme in the screenshots example. Alongside each workstream.

## Architecture decisions

### Navigation

- **A section is a `Page`, never a number.** The selected section is a `Page` value;
  one list gives the sidebar its order and groups, and the marker's place is worked
  out from that list. The Rust side (history, openers, tests) uses `Page` too, and
  the position table in `navigation.rs` goes. Reason: decided in the listening spec;
  a new first section would otherwise shift every call site again.
- **Home's Show all pages are places** in the history, like an album open in Albums:
  Back returns to Home, and the sidebar's Home entry shows Home itself.
- **A card opened from Home returns to Home,** as a Search result returns to Search.
  The "opened from search" state becomes "opened from" a page, so the back button,
  its label and Escape serve both.

### Queries

- **No schema change.** The queries read `plays`, `tracks`, `albums` and
  `favourites` as they are, and the new settings are key-value rows. Reason: nothing
  to renumber against other schema changes.
- **The library never parses an origin; the app resolves it.** The library hands
  back recent plays with their origin text and the played track's current album. The
  app, which makes the keys, turns each into an entry. The key format gets one owner
  that the player service, the agents' reader and Home share; today it is written in
  two files and parsed in both (the queue panel's "Next from" and the agents'
  `context`). Reason: the play history spec keeps the text opaque in storage, and a
  third reader would drift.
- **Agents see no change.** Plays from Home's lists report no context to MCP clients,
  as plays from unknown origins do today; no MCP tool is added or changed.
- **Recently played entries** (the owner's rule):

  | Origin of the play | Entry |
  |---|---|
  | `album:<id>` | The track's current album; the stored id is not used |
  | `artist:<id>`, `playlist:<id>`, `label:<id>` | That artist, playlist or label, while it exists |
  | `favourites` | Favourites |
  | Anything else (search, Songs, a folder, an agent, Home's own lists, none), or an id that no longer resolves | The track's current album |

  An entry appears once, at its newest play, newest first. Reason for "current
  album": an album gets a new id when a tag edit regroups it, and the track still
  knows where it is.
- **Recently added:** albums by the newest added date among their tracks.
- **Most played:** tracks ranked by their plays that started inside the range;
  albums ranked by the plays of their tracks, credited to the track's current album.
  The range starts 7, 30 or 365 days before the moment the query runs.
- **Forgotten favourites:** liked tracks whose last play, and whose like, are both
  more than 90 days old (the owner's reading of the 3-month rule).
- **Files that are gone are left out** of every query, as on the library pages.

### Home

- **Rows.** A card section shows as many cards as fit one row at the window's width,
  by the grid's own column rule, with Show all in its heading row. Forgotten
  favourites is the usual track list, a few rows long.
- **Cards in Recently played** reuse what exists: albums square with the album menu,
  artists round, playlists and Favourites as on the Playlists grid. A label has no
  card today, so it gets one, awaiting the owner's review. A card opens its page; it
  does not start playback.
- **The daily pick** orders the forgotten favourites by a shuffle seeded with the
  local date: the same all day and after a restart, and testable for a given date.
  A track that gets played drops out and the next of that day's order takes its
  place. Show all lists every one, longest unplayed first.
- **Home's lists are play contexts.** Forgotten favourites and Most played's top
  tracks each have an origin key and a name in the queue panel. Home's short list
  queues the rows it shows; a Show all list queues all of it.
- **Refresh.** The sections are read when Home is shown, after a play is recorded
  and after the library reloads, only while Home or one of its Show all pages is on
  screen. Hearts change in place, as on every list.
- **Most played's heading and picker stay** when the range is empty, with a line
  saying so. Home's page-wide empty states follow the other pages.

### Start page

- **`ui.start_page`** is `home` or `last`, a two-way choice in Settings, Appearance.
  Without the setting it is `home`, for existing libraries too.
- **The last place is a saved setting** naming a section, or the album, artist,
  playlist, label, Library check or Home list open in it. It is reopened through the
  openers the history already uses, once the library is loaded. A place that is gone
  (a deleted playlist, an album regrouped under a new id) opens Home.
- **What is not a place stays out:** the now playing view, the queue panel (it has
  its own setting), dialogs, the Search text and scroll positions.

### What tests enforce

- **Library:** each query at its range boundary; album credit after a regroup; gone
  files skipped; every case of the forgotten rule; an empty log.
- **App:** every origin kind resolves to its entry, a deleted playlist or label falls
  back to the album, entries are unique and newest first; the daily pick is stable
  for a date and differs for another; the start page for `home`, `last` and a last
  place that is gone; Home refreshes after a recorded play and not on position
  events; no source or test selects a section by position.
- **UI invariants** as today (`@tr()`, no colour literals, keyboard): each card row
  and the track list is one tab stop, Show all and the picker are reachable, and the
  smallest window keeps Most played's heading row inside.

## Weaknesses and risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Cost | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| One PR holds 35 mechanical renames and the feature while CI is off (the owner's choice) | Certain | A Home bug hides among renames | The navigation change is built and checked first, with all checks run on it before Home is built on top |
| Window tests and the screenshots example assume the window opens on Albums; with Home as the default they start elsewhere | Certain | Low-medium: failing or misleading tests and shots | Tests and examples name the page they need; the screenshots example adds Home |
| A Songs shuffle, a folder, search or Up next puts one album per track into Recently played | High for that way of listening | Low-medium: a noisy row | Entries are unique; accepted with the owner's rule; grouping such plays is a later change |
| Recently played mixes square, round, playlist and label cards in one row | Certain | Low: uneven row | Existing card shapes; screenshots in every theme; antislop during the UI work |
| The label card is a new visual without direction from the owner | Certain | Low | Recorded in DESIGN.md under "Awaiting the owner's review" |
| Album ranking by the sum of track plays favours long albums: a long compilation played once outranks an EP played five times | Medium | Low-medium: an odd order | Documented in ui.md; the measure is one query to change; the statistics page can rank differently |
| The daily pick hides a track that was there yesterday | Certain | Low | Show all lists every one in a fixed order |
| Home is sparse at first: play history began with 26.11, Forgotten favourites needs 3 months of hearts | High | Quiet Home | Empty sections are hidden; Recently added is always filled; the Most played picker stays |
| Existing users land on Home instead of Albums after updating | Certain | Low: surprise | The setting; named in the changelog |
| The last place is reopened before the library is loaded, or names something gone | Medium | Low-medium: a flash of the wrong page | Reopen after the first load; fall back to Home; a test for a place that is gone |
| Home's queries run on the UI thread after every counted play, over a log that only grows | Low now, rising | Medium: a stall | The log's time index; bounded results; refresh only while Home is shown; measure with a large synthetic log before merging |
| Most played's heading row holds a title, a four-way picker and Show all at 720 px | Medium | Low: clipped controls | The smallest-window test; the picker may take a narrower form there |
| A third reader of origin keys drifts from the writer | Medium | Medium: wrong cards | One owner for the format; a round-trip test for every kind |
| Server tracks (the agreed, unbuilt streaming server) would reach Home through the merged library with nothing planned for their greyed state | Low now | Low | Revisited when that feature is built |

**Questions deliberately left open**

- **Sizes:** how many rows Home's track list has (about five) and where the Show all
  pages of Recently played and Recently added stop (proposed: 100 entries). Chosen
  during implementation.
- **Whether the Albums/Tracks switch on Most played's Show all is remembered.**
  Proposed: no, it opens on Albums, which is what Home's row showed.
- **The look of the label card and the second line of each Recently played card.**
  Settled during the UI work and recorded in DESIGN.md for the owner's review.

## Out of scope

- From the listening spec: sideways-scrolling shelves, recommendations beyond the
  four sections, much-played albums in Forgotten favourites, custom date ranges, a
  statistics page, pausing play history, and MCP tools for play history or Home.
- The setup wizard: no start-page step and no change to its Welcome text.
- Top tracks on Home itself, and cards for tracks (the owner's choices).
- A play button on Home's cards; cards open pages, as everywhere.
- Sorting the Albums page by added date or by plays.
- Any schema change, and any change to what the play log stores (an origin for Up
  next entries, for one).
- Grouping the single-track albums a shuffle leaves in Recently played.
- Remembering the Search text or scroll positions with the last place.
