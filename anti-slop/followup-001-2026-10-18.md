# antislop follow-up 001: what came of audit 001

Date: 2026-10-18. Follows `audit-001-2026-10-18.md`; the numbers are its findings.

## Fixed

| # | Finding | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Muted text on a hovered row fails contrast in the light theme | Rows filled for hover now turn their secondary text to `text`, as selected rows already did: track lists, the Songs table and its headings, folder rows and the path above them, queue rows, and the now playing view's "Next". The contrast test also checks accent text on `surface` (the playing title on a filled row). Light `text-muted` was already Catppuccin's darkest subtext, so it could not simply be darkened. |
| 2 | The queue panel covers primary actions under 1000 px | The owner's choice: in a narrower window the panel covers the whole page area (and the now playing view) from the sidebar to the window's edge; the sidebar stays. Once it has slid in, the pages under it are hidden, so Tab can't reach controls nobody can see. Escape closes it. Tested at 900 and 1200 px. |
| 4 | The tag editor marks a wrong value in the accent | The owner's choice: Catppuccin Red, as a new `error` token (Latte `#d20f39`, Mocha `#f38ba8`). The wrong field's label is bold and red, and the sentence under the form that names the field is red too. Latte red passes 4.5:1 on base only (4.80:1; 4.46:1 on mantle), so errors are written on base, and the contrast test checks that pair. |
| 6 | Radii in the docs and the code differ | The rule in `docs/ui.md` now names the round shapes (icon-only buttons, the play button, progress and slider tracks, the knob, drop lines). The slider, its knob, both drop lines and the column edge take half their own size instead of pixel literals, and `tests/ui_invariants.rs` refuses a radius written in bare pixels. |
| 7 | Empty states point elsewhere | Artists, Songs and Folders now offer the same "Add music folder…" button as Albums, and the doubled "in in" is gone. Songs says "No songs found in your music folders." when folders exist but hold no tracks. |
| 8 | The identity rests on one 3 px marker | The owner's choice: no neon. The glow around the page marker is gone, and the `glow` token with it. Recorded in `docs/DESIGN.md`. |
| 10 | The now playing view stops growing at 560 px | The cover grows to 880 px, within the 1200 px it is decoded at, and the title steps from 34 to 42 px when the view is wider than 1800 px. |
