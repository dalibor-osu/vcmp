# antislop audit 001: VCMP UI design review

Date: 2026-10-18. Mode: after (an audit of the finished UI). Nothing in the UI was
changed for this report.

## How this was checked

- **Direction:** `docs/DESIGN.md` (dials ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2, Catppuccin
  palette, Catppuccin Pink as the single neon accent, Sora) and the recorded R-01
  owner decision. `docs/ui.md` was read for the reasons behind each choice.
- **Code:** every `.slint` file under `crates/vcmp-app/ui/` was searched for colour,
  accent, glow, shadow, radius, motion and copy patterns. The palettes are in
  `src/theme.rs`.
- **Screenshots:** `--example screenshots` rendered against the owner's real library
  (3,621 tracks, scanned read-only into `target/scan-example`), in both themes, at two
  window sizes:
  - **Run 1:** 936 x 1028 px, half of a 1920 px screen, given by the tiling window
    manager.
  - **Run 2:** 2536 x 1394 px, the whole 2560 x 1440 screen with nothing else open.
    This is the size most people will see.

  Both runs were built from a clean copy of `HEAD` (9511394), because the uncommitted
  track-clicks changes in the working tree are still in progress (the first run's
  attempt failed at `library_view.rs:931`, `AlbumDetail` has no `track_artists`).
- **Contrast:** WCAG 2 formula, the same one `src/theme.rs` uses, run on the pairs its
  test does not cover.
- **Not done:** no click-through of a running app, and no test run (the working tree
  doesn't build). Line numbers refer to `HEAD`.

## Overall verdict

The UI is not sloppy. It avoids almost every pattern in the antislop catalogue, and
nearly every decision has a written reason in `docs/ui.md`. That is rare.

What it already gets right:

- **Colour:** no gradients, glassmorphism, background grids or shadows used for
  decoration. The one `drop-shadow` in the UI is the glow on the current-page marker
  (`app-window.slint:400`), which matches the owner's "subtle neon".
- **Palette:** a real system: nine tokens per theme, no colour literals outside
  `theme.slint` (enforced by a test), and both themes contrast-tested.
- **Icons and type:** the icons are drawn for VCMP on a 24-point grid, not taken from
  Lucide. The navigation is text only. Sora was chosen by the owner, with two weights
  and a small type scale whose reason is written down.
- **Layout:** no landing-page or dashboard template. The structure follows the
  content: a cover grid, track lists, a sortable table, settings rows.
- **States:** empty, loading and error states name the cause and the next step
  ("The music library could not be opened, so no albums can be shown.", "No covers
  turned up for this album. MusicBrainz may know it under another title or artist.").
- **Honesty:** no invented numbers, people or claims. The app icon is a labelled
  placeholder until a real logo exists.
- **Copy:** no em dashes, emoji, buzzwords, decorative arrows (the back chevron is the
  only arrow, and its reason is written down) or uppercase-with-tracking labels.
- **Motion:** two durations, nothing loops, reduced motion follows the OS. This
  matches MOTION 2.
- **Keyboard:** every control has a focus ring shown only in keyboard mode, and
  grids and lists are single tab stops with arrow keys.

The findings below are what stands between this and a clean gate. Two are HIGH: a
light-theme contrast gap the tests miss, and the queue panel covering primary actions
in narrower windows.

The full-screen run (findings 9 and 10) showed the other end. On a 2560 px screen
most pages keep a 720 px column at the left edge, while their headers stretch to the
right edge. Most of the screen is then empty space that doesn't separate or group
anything.

## Findings

Priority follows the rule's tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality
Locks = LOW.

### 1. HIGH: muted text on a hovered row fails contrast in the light theme (R-25)

Secondary text keeps `text-muted` when a row fills with `surface` on hover. In Latte
that pair is **4.05:1**, under the 4.5:1 that `docs/ui.md` promises. Dark passes
(5.65:1).

The selected state already handles this: its secondary text turns to full `text`
(`songs-page.slint:139`, `album-page.slint:163`). The hovered state doesn't, so every
hover in the light theme drops below AA:

- songs table rows (`songs-page.slint:139`, `:152`) and its unsorted headers
  (`:53`, `:64`)
- every `TrackList` row: album, artist, search, folder and playlist pages
  (`album-page.slint:163`, `:184`)
- folder rows (`folders-page.slint:41`, `:72`)
- queue panel rows (`queue-panel.slint:47`, with muted text at `:121`, `:130`)
- the now playing view's "Next" label (`now-playing.slint:61`, `:70`)

The tests miss it because `src/theme.rs` checks muted text only on base and mantle.
It checks accent on surface only at the 3:1 control level, though accent is also text
on surface (the playing track's title on a hovered row, the selected dropdown option).
That pair passes at 4.61:1, but only by a little.

Fix: turn secondary text to `text` on hover as well as on selection. Alternatively,
darken the light `text-muted` until it reaches 4.5:1 on `surface`. Either way, add
"muted text on surface" and "accent text on surface" to the text pairs in
`assert_pairs`.

### 2. HIGH: in windows under 1000 px the queue panel hides primary actions (R-03, C-4)

`docs/ui.md` says this on purpose: below 1000 px the panel lies over the page's right
edge "so the page keeps the width its controls need". At 936 px (half of a 1920 px
screen) the result is that controls and text the page needs are covered:

- **Album page:** the title is cut to "POP/STAR", the artist to "(여자)아이들 , Mad",
  and the Shuffle button is half hidden (`album-light.png`).
- **Artist page:** the name is cut mid-word, and Shuffle is half hidden
  (`artist-dark.png`).
- **Playlists page, empty:** "New playlist" isn't visible at all. The empty-state
  sentence that tells you to use it is cut off at "or fron" (`playlist-light.png`).
- **Selection:** selected rows run under the panel, so their right-hand columns can't
  be seen (`selection-light.png`).

The keyboard can still tab to a covered button, and its focus ring is then hidden
behind the panel (R-32). Closing the panel brings the controls back, but nothing on
the page says they are there.

Fix (needs an owner decision): the panel could push the page whenever the page still
has its minimum width after the push. The page header could wrap its actions under the
title. Or, below the threshold, the panel could act as an overlay with a scrim that
Escape closes, so it reads as temporary rather than as part of the layout. The layout
test that checks "nothing leaves a 720 px wide window" should then also cover a
window just under 1000 px with the panel open.

Run 2 confirms that this happens only in narrower windows. At 2536 px the panel pushes
the page and nothing is covered (`album-light.png`, `artist-light.png`).

### 3. MEDIUM: the accent has many jobs, and two big pink shapes compete on track pages (R-01, R-31, one deliberate accent)

`docs/ui.md` gives pink one job: "marks what is active". It actually does six:

- the current page
- what plays now (the play button, the seek fill, the playing title and mark, and the
  queue's "Now playing")
- switched-on state (switches, the segmented choice, shuffle and repeat)
- the primary action on a page (Play)
- keyboard focus
- the tag editor's wrong field (see 4)

Each job has a reason, so this is not "accent everywhere" by default. But on the
album, artist and playlist pages, the filled Play button and the filled play/pause
button are the two largest pink shapes, and they compete as the focal point. With the
queue open, the light album page shows about eight pink elements at once
(`album-light.png`). Settings shows five, before the player bar (`settings-light.png`).

The light accent (`#a1197d`) is a deep magenta, not a pastel, so the density is more
noticeable in Latte than in Mocha.

Fix (owner decision): choose the focal element per screen. For example, keep the
page's Play pink and draw the bar's play/pause in `text` on `surface` while a page
offers its own Play. Or keep the bar's button pink and make the page's Play outlined.
Write the rule that results into the "Accent on the play button…" row of
`docs/ui.md`.

### 4. MEDIUM: the tag editor marks a wrong value in the accent colour (R-01, R-31)

`tag-editor.slint:119` turns the label of a wrong field pink and bold. Everywhere
else, pink means active, playing or on, so here it reads as "this field is selected"
rather than "this field is wrong". The sentence under the form does name the field,
so the information is still there.

Fix (owner decision, because DESIGN.md has a single accent): keep the label in `text`
and mark the field with words ("Year: not a year") or an error glyph. Alternatively,
add an `error` token (Catppuccin Red or Maroon, contrast-tested in both themes) and
record that a second hue is allowed for errors only.

### 5. LOW: `docs/ui.md` no longer matches the buttons (R-31)

The design decisions table says: "Accent-filled button only for 'Add music folder…' on
the empty Albums page; every other button is outlined."

The code also fills these buttons with the accent:

- Play on the album page (`album-page.slint:813`)
- Play on the artist page (`artist-page.slint:217`)
- two buttons on the playlist page (`playlist-page.slint:203`, `:246`)
- Write N files and the save buttons in the tag editor (`tag-editor.slint:547`, `:577`)

CLAUDE.md requires the docs to change with the code.

Fix: update the row to the rule the code follows ("the one action a page or form
exists for"), or bring the code back to the documented rule. Decide this together
with finding 3.

### 6. LOW: the radius rule in the docs and the radii in the code differ (R-11)

`docs/ui.md` says "Radii 2 / 8 / 12 px, pill only for the switch". In the code:

- The play button, `IconButton`'s hover fill and `ProgressBar` are also fully round
  (`self.height / 2`). Circles for icon buttons are a sound choice, so the docs are
  what's wrong here.
- The slider uses literal radii instead of the tokens: `2px` and a `6px` knob in
  `player-bar.slint:118`, `:127` and `:136`. So does the drop line, with `1px` in
  `queue-panel.slint:475`.

Fix: write down "round for icon-only buttons and progress tracks". Then use the
tokens, or a `radius-round` token, for the slider and the drop line.

### 7. LOW: some empty states point elsewhere instead of offering the action (R-27, UI skill "Placeholder empty states")

- **Albums page, empty:** has the one primary button, "Add music folder…".
- **Songs, Artists and Folders pages, empty:** say "Add the folder your music is in in
  Settings" with no button (`pages.slint:554`, `:999`; `folders-page.slint:255`). The
  doubled "in in" reads like a typo.

Fix: reword, for example "Add your music folder in Settings". Better, put the same
"Add music folder…" button on these pages, or a link that opens Settings at Music
folders. This is a copy and empty-state change, so check it against finding 3 if it
adds a primary button.

### 8. LOW: the identity rests on one 3 px marker (R-20, identity motif)

The UI has a clear identity in dark mode. In light mode the glow around the page
marker barely shows on the mantle (Latte pink at 69%), so the one neon gesture almost
disappears. What's left is a tidy pastel layout that could belong to any player.

DESIGN.md asks for "cyberpunk-ish neon, but subtle" and allows neon on 1 to 2 focal
elements per screen, but only one is used. This is not slop. It is room the direction
leaves open.

Fix (owner decision, design not code): either accept that light mode is calmer, and
write that down, or give the neon one more focal element within the agreed dose. The
glow could go on the play/pause button while playing, or on the edge of the now
playing cover. No looping pulse: MOTION 2 forbids it.

### 9. MEDIUM: on a full-screen window, the pages have no width rule of their own (structural whitespace, R-05, C-3)

`Theme.content-max-width` (720 px, `theme.slint:79`) caps nearly every page's content,
pinned to the left edge. `docs/ui.md` gives one reason for the cap: "Keeps a setting's
label and its control within one glance on wide windows". That fits Settings. Every
other page uses the same cap, and in run 2 it leaves about 1,800 of 2,360 px of the
content area empty:

- **Album page:** a 200 px cover and a two-track list in the top-left corner, and the
  rest of the page empty (`album-light.png`). The track list's durations end at 720 px.
- **Artist page:** the name and its single album card sit in the top-left quarter
  (`artist-light.png`).
- **Tag editor:** the form is 470 px wide, at the top left of a 2,536 px overlay
  (`tags-light.png`).
- **Search:** the field and results stop at 720 px. The scrollbar sits at the window's
  right edge, 1,200 px from the list it scrolls (`search-light.png`).

The page headers don't follow the cap, so header actions end up far from the content
they belong to:

- **Playlists, empty:** the sentence says "Make one with New playlist". The button is
  at the top right, about 1,700 px away from the sentence (`playlists-dark.png`).
- **Albums, Artists, Songs:** the filter field sits at the far right, while the title
  is at the far left.

The Songs table is the opposite case. It spans the full width, so the eye travels
about 2,300 px from a title to its duration, with nothing on the row to follow
(`songs-dark.png`).

Under the liveliness bar, whitespace should be structure, not leftover space. Here it
is leftover space: it neither separates nor groups anything. The cause is one cap
applied to every page, so it is not a template.

Fix (owner decision, since it changes the layout): give each kind of page its own rule
and write it into `docs/ui.md`:

- **Reading width for forms:** Settings, the tag editor and empty-state text. Keep the
  720 px cap, but centre the column or put the header actions inside it.
- **Track pages:** album, artist, playlist, search and folders. A wider cap (around
  1,200 px), with the track list's columns growing as on the Songs page, and a cover
  that scales with the width (for example 200 to 300 px).
- **Grids and the Songs table:** full width, as now. The table needs a maximum column
  width or a centred cap, and its rows a stronger hover so the eye can follow a row
  across.

Then add a test or screenshot check at 2560 px, the way 720 px is covered now.

### 10. LOW: the now playing view stops growing at 560 px (one focal point per screen)

The view exists to show the cover large. `now-playing.slint:199` caps it at 560 px, so
on the full screen it takes 40% of the height. The title next to it (34 px) and the
metadata are small for the space, and the group floats in the middle of an empty
screen (`now-playing-dark.png`).

The cap protects image quality, since the large cover is decoded at up to 1,200 px
(`docs/ui.md`). Raising it to about 800 to 900 px stays within that.

Fix: raise the cap in line with the decode size, and let the title scale a step with
it (for example `font-display` at widths above about 1,800 px). Record the reason
next to the cap.
